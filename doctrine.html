MANDATORY // THE NOVA BRIGADE

THE RECRUITMENT MANUAL

Official Training Document — status: MANDATORY FOR ALL RECRUIT TRAINING — improper use will be punished.

The Set Up

  1. Get your recruit to the yellow PT lines.
  2. Get your recruit in the Nova Union and Nova Brigade VRChat groups. Make sure they are representing the Nova Brigade VRChat group.
  3. Give your recruit the proper status: “E-1 | Nickname | T.N.B”.
  4. Give your recruit the enlisted avatar.

The Training Process

  1. Teach your recruit the proper faces: left face, right face, about face, center face, and command face.
  2. Teach your recruit their comms: black comms, red comms, green comms.

The Important Parts

Make sure your recruit understands the following:

  • Follow all orders.
  • Represent The Nova Brigade professionally.
  • Do not speak over your high command.
  • Never falsely claim a rank or position.

The Obstacle Course

Ask the recruit if they would like to run through the obstacle course. It is completely optional.

  • If the answer is no — move to the final steps.
  • If the answer is yes — run through the obstacle course, then move to the final steps.

The Final Steps

  1. Get your recruit into the Discord and make sure they role-request.
  2. Give your recruit a post.

OFFICIAL // THE NOVA GOVERNMENT

THE LEGION ACT

Official Document of The Nova Government — transcribed from the official signed document.

I. DIVISION SIZE & MEMBERSHIP

  • Each Legion is limited to a maximum of 15 members.
  • Divisions must maintain active membership and regular participation.
  • Inactive divisions may be disbanded and removed from the Nova Union.

II. RANKING STRUCTURE

  • Each Legion may have a maximum of 8 ranks.
  • Upon joining, members operate exclusively under their Legion rank.
  • Nova Brigade military ranks no longer apply while serving in a Legion.
  • Promotions must be handled exclusively through the division’s leadership.

III. UNIFORM REGULATIONS

  • Every Legion must establish an official designated uniform.
  • All members, including COs and XOs, must wear the designated uniform while on duty.
  • No rank or position is exempt from this requirement.

IV. LOYALTY AGREEMENT

  • All members must serve a minimum of 14 days (2 weeks) after joining a Legion.
  • Members may not voluntarily leave before completing this period.
  • Early departures may result in disciplinary action against the division’s leadership.

V. ACTIVITY & DISBANDMENT

  • Legions are required to remain active and regularly gather their members.
  • Divisions that fail to maintain sufficient activity may be disbanded by the Government.
  • Upon disbandment, all members return to their previous Nova Brigade military ranks.

VI. COMBAT EVENT AUTHORIZATION

  • Legion combat events must receive authorization from division leadership or the Government.
  • Authorization is not required when a Legion combat event takes place alongside an officially hosted Nova Brigade combat event.

VII. DIVISIONAL AUTHORITY

  • Legions operate under their own designated leadership and ranking structures.
  • Members are subject to their specialized division rank rather than their former Nova Brigade rank.
  • No outside military authority may issue promotions to members within a Legion.

Classification: OFFICIAL — THE NOVA GOVERNMENT.

IN EFFECT // THE VICE CHANCELLOR

PROMOTIONAL STANDARDS

Official directive of the Vice Chancellor — status: IN EFFECT.

Promotional Wait Times

  • Enlisted can only be promoted once every TWO days, and ONLY by one of the three Presidents.
  • Officers can only be promoted once every FIVE days, and ONLY by one of the three Presidents.
  • Generals can only be promoted once every SEVEN days, and ONLY by one of the three Presidents.

Combat Events

  • Each time will have the best by performance promoted — NOT everyone.
  • You cannot be promoted EVERY SINGLE COMBAT EVENT — this exists to make it fair.

Demotions

  • Officers and Generals who cannot do their job(s) will be demoted from now on.
  • Generals and Senior Officers MUST train/enhance new officers to be productive, or they will be demoted.
