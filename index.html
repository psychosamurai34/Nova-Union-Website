SECTION 05

NATIONAL HONORS_

Every Thursday. No combat. Ten names called before the Union.

THURSDAY
OF THE
UNION

What it is. Every Thursday, combat operations stand down. The Union convenes for National Honors: a formal ceremony where ten members — officers, enlisted, and staff alike — are called out by name for their hard work, loyalty, and service.

Why Thursday. Because a Union that never stops fighting forgets why it fights. One night a week, the guns go quiet and the names are read.

How names are chosen. Squad leaders and division chiefs nominate. Nominations close Wednesday at 23:59. The command reviews conduct, attendance, and deeds — frontline heroics and quiet logistics miracles alike.

Nominate someone: bring their callsign and one line of why to the honors nominations channel before Wednesday 23:59.
