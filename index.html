NOVANISM_

The Official Political Ideology of The Nova Union

Novanism is an authoritarian, state-centred political ideology founded upon the principles of national sovereignty, centralized authority, national unity, discipline, loyalty, and military strength. It advocates for a powerful and independent state governed by a centralized executive leadership, where national interests take precedence over individual political ambitions.

Novanism maintains that the strength of a nation is determined by the unity, dedication, and discipline of its citizens. It emphasizes loyalty to the nation above loyalty to individual leaders, while maintaining a strict governmental hierarchy and an established political order.

In foreign affairs, Novanism promotes diplomatic pragmatism, strategic independence, and the protection of national sovereignty. Diplomatic resolution is preferred over unnecessary confrontation, although decisive action remains an option when national security and sovereignty are threatened.

The ultimate objective of Novanism is to establish and maintain the Nova Union as a powerful, independent, disciplined, and internationally respected nation.

The History of The Nova Union

From a Small Corporation to VRChat's Largest Milsim Organization

The history of The Nova Union began with the founding of The Nova Corporation, established by an individual named Niko. After several months of operation, the organization underwent a major transformation, rebranding into The Nova Empire. This marked the first time Nova became an official state, establishing its own identity, leadership, and structure.

During the early days of the Empire, Kaped joined the organization. Through dedication and hard work, he climbed through the ranks and eventually became Vice President of The Nova Empire, playing an important role in its development and future.

Following the Empire's success, The Nova Union was founded, marking another major chapter in Nova's history and the beginning of its rise as a major force within the VRChat Milsim community.

Throughout its existence, Nova faced numerous national crises, internal struggles, and difficult periods. Yet, despite every obstacle, its people never gave up. Members remained loyal, continued believing in the organization, and worked together to ensure its survival and growth.

Today, what once started as a small corporation has evolved into the largest Milsim organization on VRChat. Its journey is a testament to the dedication, ambition, and loyalty of the people who built it.

Nova has changed names, leadership, and generations, but its legacy has never faded.
